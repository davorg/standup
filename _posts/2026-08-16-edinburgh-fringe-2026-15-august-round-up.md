**Saturday 15 August** brought the Fringe's second weekend and another varied
set of reviews. Our team was back in London, following a festival that still
had more than two weeks to run.

## Reviews elsewhere

The Guardian's reviews today ranged across several definitions of comedy:

- **Charlene Kaye – *Diversity Shredder*** combines a sharp account of the
  toxic music industry with live guitar and killer riffs.
- **Ayo Adenekan – *Homeland Insecurity*** gives the SNL UK writer a warm,
  romantic showcase.
- **A Foot Is Not an Appropriate Prize for the Tombola** turns one disastrous
  day at a village fete into comedy.
- **Flamenc Oh!!** mixes fierce footwork, flirting and breezy physical humour.

They are all linked from the Guardian's
[comedy coverage](https://www.theguardian.com/stage/comedy).

## Looking ahead

These daily round-ups will continue from London. There are reviews still to
read, names still to emerge and Comedy Awards shortlists to argue about before
the month is over.
