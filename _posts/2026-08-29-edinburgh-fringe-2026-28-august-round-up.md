**Friday 28 August** was not the main Edinburgh Comedy Awards day, but it was
far from quiet. The final weekend began with two sets of winners, one new
Scottish comedy prize and a pair of late Guardian reviews for official
newcomer nominees.

## ISH announces its winners

The
[ISH Edinburgh Comedy Awards 2026 winners](https://www.comedy.co.uk/fringe/news/8833/ish-edinburgh-comedy-awards-2026-winners/)
were announced at a late-night ceremony, with **Harriet Dyer** and **Paul
Foot** sharing Best Show. The Best Newcomer prize went to **Sam Eley** for
*Basil Crumbwick: Soul Sewage*, while **Matthew Gallagher** won Best Joke.

That result makes this year's awards picture more complicated in a useful
way. Dyer is also on the official Edinburgh Comedy Awards Best Show
shortlist, while Eley missed the official Newcomer list despite appearing in
several other late-festival conversations. ISH has always existed partly to
catch what another judging structure might miss, and this year's split view
shows why that matters.

## Conk wins The List

The List Festival Awards named
**[Conk's *Man Sings The Same Song Over And Over Again For An Hour*](https://www.comedy.co.uk/fringe/fyi/1163/conk-wins-at-list-festival-awards/)**
as **Best Comedy Show**. The premise still sounds like a dare, but over the
month it has moved from oddity to genuine word-of-mouth hit.

The
[full List Festival Awards winners](https://list.co.uk/news/winners-announced-for-the-list-festival-awards-2026-49041)
also included **Megasquirt: Fully Loaded** as Best LGBTQIA+ Show and
**Sarah Hester Ross: Serving C*nt** as Best Cabaret Show.

## A first McTavish winner

The inaugural McTavish Award went to
**[Chris Forbes - *Father Christmas*](https://www.chortle.com/news/2026/08/29/61498/chris_forbes_named_best_scottish_comic_at_the_edinburgh_fringe)**.
The new prize, created in memory of Vladimir McTavish, recognises an
outstanding show by a Scottish or Scottish-based act.

It is hard not to read that alongside the official Edinburgh Comedy Awards
shortlists, which again contain no Scottish-based act. The McTavish Award is
not a consolation prize; its practical package of money, a Stand run or tour
and development mentoring gives it a different job. But it does point towards
a gap that the bigger awards have not filled.

## Reviews of the day

The Guardian's 28 August reviews focused on two official newcomer nominees
and one wider-Fringe political spectacle:

- **[Li Jin Hao - *Falling From a Moon*](https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2026/aug/28/li-jin-hao-falling-from-a-moon-review-pleasance-courtyard-edinburgh)**
  gets a warm review for a reflective hour about nostalgia, migration,
  language and learning not to live entirely in the past. Li is up for Best
  Comedy Show at Saturday's Edinburgh Comedy Awards.

- **[Tom Towelling Teaches You to Knit](https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2026/aug/28/tom-towelling-teaches-you-to-knit-review-pleasance-courtyard-edinburgh)**
  is treated as an eccentric debut full of classroom energy, audience
  interaction and wool-based whimsy. Towelling is on the Best Newcomer
  shortlist, and the review suggests the oddness is both the point and the
  risk.

- **[Clown Show](https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2026/aug/28/clown-show-review-kings-theatre-edinburgh)**
  takes clowning into much darker territory, turning the 250th anniversary of
  the United States into a theatrical collapse of slapstick, national myth and
  political dread.

## Before the big one

The official Edinburgh Comedy Awards winners are due on **Saturday 29
August**. That means today is the last moment when all the possible outcomes
remain suspended: Lara Ricote could complete a widely predicted run, Dan
Tiernan could repeat last year's momentum, Harriet Dyer could convert ISH
approval into the official prize, or the judges could swerve towards Li Jin
Hao, Chris Cantrill or Spencer Jones.

The Newcomer field feels just as open. Friday's Guardian attention on Tom
Towelling keeps one nominee visible, while Freddie Meredith, Marty Gleeson,
Omar Badawy, Fatiha El-Ghorri, Dom McGovern and UNDERGROUND MONK SHOW all
have their own arguments.

## Today's takeaway

Friday did what the penultimate day should do: it complicated the story. Conk
won The List, Dyer and Foot shared ISH, Forbes gave the McTavish Award a
strong first winner, and the official Comedy Awards still have the final word
to come.
