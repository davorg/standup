**Monday 24 August** was a useful final-week snapshot: established stand-up,
queer double-act intensity, clowning, storytelling and peer-voted awards all
arrived in the same news cycle. The Fringe is now close enough to its ending
that every review and shortlist can change the last few days of ticket sales.

## Reviews of the day

The Guardian's comedy reviews kept circling shows that have already appeared
in awards conversation:

- **[Dan Tiernan - *Quartz and All*](https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2026/aug/24/dan-tiernan-quartz-and-all-review-monkey-barrel-edinburgh)**
  finds the Mancunian comic turning to healing crystals in search of peace,
  while remaining as frenetic, self-lacerating and physically explosive as
  ever. It sounds like a show powered by the gap between the calm he claims to
  want and the stage energy he cannot help producing.

- **[Those People](https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2026/aug/24/those-people-review-pleasance-courtyard-edinburgh)**
  gives Lara Ciulli and Riana White a slippery hour of sketch, dance and
  confessional comedy about queer female friendship. The review is especially
  interested in how close the pair's bickering feels to real intimacy.

- **[Frankie Thompson - *Horrible Things*](https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2026/aug/24/frankie-thompson-horrible-things-review)**
  uses clowning, lip-sync and performance art to exorcise unsettling scraps
  of British cultural memory. Jacob Rees-Mogg, Frank Sinatra and male ego all
  become raw material for something much stranger than a conventional satirical
  hour.

The day's wider festival coverage also included
**[Goodbye Dandelion](https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2026/aug/24/goodbye-dandelion-review-ode-to-a-fabulous-friendship-with-a-50-year-age-gap)**,
Maddie and Marcus Rice's storytelling show about an intergenerational
friendship, assisted dying and the life lessons passed from one woman to
another.

## The comedians' shortlist

Chortle reported the
[2026 Comedians' Choice Awards shortlists](https://www.chortle.co.uk/news/2026/08/24/61424/shortlists_announced_for_comedians%E2%80%99_choice_awards_2026),
voted for by performers at the Fringe. The Best Show list includes **Dan
Tiernan - *Quartz and All***, **Elf Lyons - *The Woman on the Edge***, **Kate
Dolan - *Trout***, **Lara Ricote - *Inkling***, **Sid Singh - *Love Bombing***
and **Tom Short - *I Get ChatGPT To Write My Edinburgh Show***.

The Best Newcomer shortlist includes **Ben McCarthy**, **Ethan & Gigi**,
**Lady Bolognese**, **Madeleine Brettingham**, **Marty Gleeson**, **Sam Eley**
and **Sapphire McIntosh**, among others.

These awards are not the same as the Edinburgh Comedy Awards or the ISH
Awards, and that is the point. A peer-voted list catches a slightly different
kind of heat: what performers are recommending to each other after their own
flyering, tech checks and late-night post-show debriefs.

## Why 24 August matters

This is the part of the Fringe where narratives start to overlap. **Dan
Tiernan**, **Lara Ricote**, **Elf Lyons**, **Sam Eley** and **Those People**
have now appeared in multiple review or award conversations. That does not
make any of them inevitable winners, but it suggests their shows are cutting
through several different filters at once.

The more interesting lesson is the range. Healing-crystal stand-up, movement
comedy, clowning, queer friendship, storytelling and algorithm jokes are all
competing for attention in the same week.

## Today's takeaway

Final-week Edinburgh rewards momentum, but it also rewards curiosity. The
award lists are useful, the late reviews are useful, and the best plan is
still to let one of them send you somewhere you had not expected to go.
