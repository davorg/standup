**Friday 21 August** was awards day. Not the final verdict, but the point at
which the comedy conversation becomes much more concrete: the
[ISH Edinburgh Comedy Awards longlists](https://www.comedy.co.uk/fringe/fyi/1158/ish-edinburgh-comeedy-awards-2026-longlists/)
were announced.

## The ISH longlists

The 2026 ISH longlists contain **60 shows**: **39** in Best Show and **21** in
Best Newcomer. That size matters. A shortlist tells a tight story; a longlist
shows the spread of what the judges have found compelling across the month.

Best Show includes several names that have been gathering momentum throughout
the run:

- **Andrew White - *What a Life!***
- **Chris Cantrill - *Rewilding***
- **Crybabies - *The Scaring***
- **Dan Tiernan - *Quartz and All***
- **Elf Lyons - *The Woman On The Edge***
- **John Kearns - *Tilting at Windmills***
- **Joseph Morpurgo - *Highlander 70***
- **Lara Ricote - *INKLING***
- **Omar Badawy - *Guided Detour***
- **Paul Foot - *The Future***
- **Sam Eley - *Basil Crumbwick: Soul Sewage***

The Newcomer list is just as useful for anyone still trying to find emerging
voices before the festival ends. It includes **Anna Leong Brophy**, **Dane
Buckley**, **Dom McGovern**, **Freddie Meredith**, **Jack Brookmyre**,
**Jonathan Oldfield**, **Madeleine Brettingham**, **Scout Boxall**, **Tarang
Hardikar** and **Those People**.

The
[full ISH Edinburgh Comedy Awards longlists](https://www.comedy.co.uk/fringe/fyi/1158/ish-edinburgh-comeedy-awards-2026-longlists/)
are on British Comedy Guide.

## What the longlist tells us

The overlap with
[Chortle's current high-scoring reviews](https://www.chortle.co.uk/about/2026/07/20/61020/edinburgh_fringe_2026_comedy_reviews)
is striking. **Sam Eley**, **Crybabies**, **Lara Ricote**, **Andrew White**
and **Those People** all appear in the upper reaches of the specialist review
index too. That does not make them inevitable winners, but it does suggest a
shared critical temperature.

The list also keeps space for very different comic modes: big theatrical
concepts, stand-up craft, clown-adjacent strangeness, personal storytelling
and formally ambitious ensemble work. That breadth is welcome. A Fringe award
that only rewards one idea of comedy would be much less useful.

## What happens next

The ISH shortlists are due on **Wednesday 26 August**, with winners announced
on **Friday 28 August**. The official Edinburgh Comedy Awards shortlist is
also due on **Wednesday 26 August**, with its ceremony on **Saturday 29
August**.

That makes the next few days unusually compressed. Reviews are still coming,
audiences are still discovering shows and awards judges are trying to compare
work that was never designed to be compared.

## Today's takeaway

The longlist is not a map of the whole Fringe, but it is a good late-festival
route planner. If you are in Edinburgh now, this is the week to use it: once
the shortlist lands, the remaining tickets will get harder to find.
