**Wednesday 26 August** was the day the Fringe comedy conversation narrowed
sharply. After three weeks of reviews, recommendations and longlists, the
[Edinburgh Comedy Awards 2026 shortlist](https://www.comedy.co.uk/fringe/news/8824/edinburgh-comedy-awards-2026-nominees/)
was announced.

## The Edinburgh Comedy Awards shortlist

The six nominees for **Best Comedy Show** are:

- **Chris Cantrill - *Rewilding***
- **Dan Tiernan - *Quartz and All***
- **Harriet Dyer - *Easily Distra...***
- **Lara Ricote - *INKLING***
- **Li Jin Hao - *Falling From a Moon***
- **Spencer Jones - *Dogs***

The seven **Best Newcomer** nominees are:

- **Dom McGovern - *Prize Hog***
- **Fatiha El-Ghorri - *Cockney Stacking Doll***
- **Freddie Meredith - *Need a Light?***
- **Marty Gleeson - *Dog Ear***
- **Omar Badawy - *Guided Detour***
- **Tom Towelling - *Tom Towelling Teaches You to Knit***
- **UNDERGROUND MONK SHOW**

The winners of Best Show, Best Newcomer and the Panel Prize will be announced
on **Saturday 29 August**. Best Show carries a **£10,000** prize, while Best
Newcomer and the Panel Prize are worth **£5,000** each.

## What the list tells us

The Guardian's
[shortlist analysis](https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2026/aug/26/whimsical-shortlist-announced-for-edinburgh-comedy-award-2026)
calls this a particularly whimsical selection, and that feels right. This is
not a shortlist dominated by topical state-of-the-nation hours. It favours
knotty stand-up, surrealism, prop comedy, imaginative structure and oddness
used with precision.

There are also some clear late-run absences. **Elf Lyons**, **Crybabies**,
**Conk**, **Sam Eley**, **Frankie McNair** and **Bebe Cave** have all been
part of this year's critical conversation, but none made the official Comedy
Awards shortlists. That does not make their shows any less successful; it
just shows how tight the final funnel is.

## ISH names its finalists too

The
[ISH Edinburgh Comedy Awards shortlist](https://www.comedy.co.uk/fringe/news/8823/ish-edinburgh-comeedy-awards-2026-shortlists/)
also arrived on Wednesday. Its Best Show finalists include **Brennan Reece**,
**Dan Tiernan**, **Eric Rushton**, **Harriet Dyer**, **Kate Cheka**, **Lara
Ricote**, **Paul Currie**, **Paul Foot**, **Reuben Kaye** and **Tom Short**.

The Newcomer shortlist includes **Dane Buckley**, **Marty Gleeson**, **Matt
Klein**, **Matthew Gallagher**, **Sam Eley** and **Tarang Hardikar**. ISH will
announce its winners on **Friday 28 August**, alongside its Panel Prize, Best
Joke and technical awards.

The overlap is interesting. **Dan Tiernan**, **Harriet Dyer**, **Lara Ricote**
and **Marty Gleeson** now appear on both the official and ISH shortlists,
while ISH gives space to several acts who missed the official list.

## The comedians have voted

The peer-voted
[Comedians' Choice Awards 2026](https://www.comedy.co.uk/fringe/news/8826/comedians-choice-awards-2026-winners/)
were also announced. **Kate Dolan - *Trout*** won Best Show, **Marty Gleeson**
won Best Newcomer and **Claire McCluskey**, head of production at Monkey
Barrel, won Best Person.

Those prizes mean something slightly different. They are not a critics'
verdict or an industry panel decision; they are what performers chose after a
month of sharing rooms, queues, backstage corridors and late-night
recommendations.

## Reviews of the day

The Guardian's 26 August reviews gave more late-festival context:

- **[Max Olesker - *Making the Cut*](https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2026/aug/26/max-olesker-making-the-cut-review-pleasance-dome-edinburgh)**
  turns Olesker's conversion to Orthodox Judaism into a nuanced comedy about
  faith, bureaucracy, marriage and sacrifice.

- **[Jess Fuchs - *Feral*](https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2026/aug/26/jess-fuchs-feral-comedy-review-edinburgh-fringe-pleasance-courtyard)**
  gives the Australian comic a strong debut notice, praising the force of her
  unsentimental, fast-talking stand-up.

- **[Kirsty Mann - *Corpse*](https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2026/aug/26/kirsty-mann-corpse-review-summerhall-edinburgh-fringe)**
  uses haunted-house storytelling to create a comic horror show that appears
  to understand the power of doing less.

- **[Sami Abu Wardeh - *Sami Abu Wardeh Hates You*](https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2026/aug/26/sami-abu-wardeh-hates-you-review-pleasance-courtyard-edinburgh)**
  gets a four-star review for an autobiographical hour that moves his clowning
  and political comedy towards the mainstream without sanding off all its
  oddity.

## Today's takeaway

The shortlist is the headline, but the larger story is the number of parallel
filters now operating at once: official awards, ISH, peer votes, critics and
audience word of mouth. The best final-week schedule probably uses all of
them, then leaves room for one more surprise.
